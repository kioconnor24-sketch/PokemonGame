## 1. NONNEGOTIABLE overall experience and narrative tone — CONFIRMED
- A large, traditional, lively Gen 1–5-style Pokémon adventure **first**. Routes, catching, training, Gyms, exploration, individual towns' personalities, friendship, humor, ordinary side quests, secrets, ecological habitats, fishing, festivals and memorable but natural small interactions should comprise most game time.
- The crime/conspiracy strand is **intermittent and sparse**, with deliberately selective clues. **Do not** attach an omen or government secret to every town, NPC, friend scene, post-Gym beat, quest, random grunt or legendary. No constant grim/edgy atmosphere, prolonged occupation, excessive speeches, extended cutscenes, repetitive melancholy or forced tragedy.
- Reserve genuinely dramatic, mature reveals for late Averon (especially around/after Gym 6). Give unrelated supporting characters ordinary interests, local humor and adventures. Organic mixed reaction to villain reforms in **a small number** of compact scenes; the world remains traversable, shops/Centers/Gyms function.
- Dialogue must sound like actual human Pokémon characters, short and situation-based, with the player making discoveries rather than being an audience for detective exposition. `DIALOGUE_GUIDE.md` contains **older illustrative dialogues**, not final user-approved lines. Villain is restrained and persuasive, no overlong manifesto; rival self-confidence/jealousy is very subtle, not repetitive.
- Full **Averon** original first region, full **Sinnoh** second; eight Gyms apiece, first region League at remote Victory Isle, Cynthia as Sinnoh Champion, then expansive optional postgame and **Red** as *last canonical/ultimate trainer battle*, above the optional repeatable Champions' Circuit. Keep max level 100, one challenging forced Set mode, original held-item Gen V EXP Share, Mega and following Pokémon, no Z-Moves/Dynamax/Terastallization.
- Sinnoh should be a **complete second Pokémon journey**, not an endless fugitive hunt that makes earning eight badges nonsensical. Majority of Sinnoh should remain lighthearted, explore its geography/mythology. The return of an antagonist, if adopted, must be sparse, well paced and not replace Cynthia, Sinnoh gameplay or Red.
- Keep older agreed major conspiracy/raid/assault structures unless individually revised below; detailed character names, teams and precise timings in October 14 package are writer defaults, **not** owner-established immutable canon.

## 2. HIGH-CONFIDENCE Averon core and Threshold cause-and-effect
### 2.1 Three independent layers — CONFIRMED as distinct
1. **Original government Project Threshold:** a public-safety experiment to temporarily depower a dangerous Pokémon by extracting its extraordinary energy. Failure of a **filtration** stage opens an anomalous **dimensional rupture**; concentrated feedback energy kills or erases a Pokémon test subject; **Zygarde emerges** via the first rupture. The original machine was **not designed to erase the species**, and it was not the villain organization's original control-collar invention.
2. **Team Vantage's public ideology:** **end humanity's dependence on Pokémon** through human-controlled infrastructure/reforms. Initially appears as a mundane Team Rocket-style criminal group. They have infiltrated government, League, police, selected Gym Leaders and other authority positions over time. They eventually stage a **successful regional government coup**, genuinely implement some promised changes and receive a *small, mixed* set of public reactions. Some rank-and-file genuinely believe the public mission; petty crimes and independent interests coexist with major operations.
3. **Leader's concealed goal:** **eliminate Pokémon entirely**, knowingly drastic and wrong. His deep *personal* reason and original ideology **MUST PRECEDE** the Threshold accident (latest explicit owner correction). Witnessing the experiment teaches him that Pokémon-targeting lethal dimensional feedback can potentially be **weaponized**, not *why* Pokémon should be removed. He manipulates the organization and staged coup to seize the altered Threshold machine/government infrastructure and scale a Pokémon-erasure burst.
- **Reason for lab robbery—CONFIRMED:** steal classified **Threshold blueprints/schematics** necessary to understand and adapt the machine, **not** merely steal its physical parts. The theft's interpretation and timing are modifiable.
- **Device climax—CONFIRMED:** during the final struggle, damage, failure or uncontrolled activation creates a powerful spatial/dimensional rupture that **pulls player and antagonist into the alternate dimension**, rather than carrying out the intended extinction explosion. A battle between player and leader **inside the dimension** is planned. The exact rupture sequence, how and when antagonist may escape, and technical resolution still need coherent explanation.
- **Unsolved science:** why Zygarde appears at first rift; why player/party Pokémon survive inside other dimension when machine killed subject; why Pokémon-specific lethal phase differs from human effects; how weapon would scale; how portal works and how each character returns. The older bible's explanation (localized, concentrated filter feedback, not ambient dimensional air; Zygarde ecological boundary response; possible Giratina return) is a **candidate explanation**, not proof/final law. Scientists can present hypotheses; avoid technobabble.
- **Important latest correction:** Do not claim Vale became anti-Pokémon solely because he saw a Pokémon killed in the first Threshold accident; user wants **his own earlier defining life experience**, still to be designed.

### 2.2 Control technology / public-facing program — PREFERRED PROVISIONAL NEW WORK
- Proposed recognizable **Pokémon-control collars, restraints or devices** first developed/tested in Averon by Team Vantage. Public-facing pitch may be humane restraint of dangerous Pokémon, greater human autonomy, safer infrastructure, and independence from uncontrolled Pokémon power. **Owner suggested** control devices **could become the organization's apparent upfront goal**; the exact public slogan/relationship to the existing LOCKED independence ideology is **OPEN**. Do not silently replace the explicit public mission until owner decides.

The organization uses/tests prototypes in relatively ordinary operations; visual collars could give consistency. Whether all uses are sinister or there is genuine rescue value is a question, not a decision.
- **Lab-raid misdirection—owner-proposed and liked:** by then the player expects thieves to be seeking better **control-device technology**. The organization raids a lab, and it seems they want collar research; actually they seek **classified Threshold blueprints**. A late discovery reinterprets what was taken; avoid clues so obvious that the deception fails. It may be valuable to recover visible unrelated equipment as cover, but that is OPTIONAL, not established.
- **Early technical limitation candidate:** collars work on ordinary Pokémon but **are unreliable on very powerful Legendary Pokémon**. A short failed control attempt on an unusually strong Pokémon could set up the later Sinnoh breakthrough. This is an assistant-proposed demonstration, not approved location/species/scene.
- **Exact device form OPEN:** physical collar in Averon vs remote control field; optionally a physical restraint early with remote/manifestation-specific application later. **Do not assume** a literal collar fits Arceus. How it affects consent, welfare, battles, Pokémon-catching and gameplay remains unresolved.
- **Villain commanders with lower-tier legends—PREFERRED early idea:** Registeel, Raikou, Silvally/Type: Null, not Arceus/Palkia/Kyogre etc on ordinary commander teams.

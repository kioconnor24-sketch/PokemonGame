## Delivery admission and routing

Before implementation, record five fields in the existing owning PR or scoped #15 claim; use the existing PR/task templates, not another queue:

1. **Outcome:** the needed playable connection/map or reproduced defect and its observable result.
2. **Reuse and gap:** canonical identities, current main status, existing source/PR and full SHA, and exactly what is still missing. Reuse existing output; do not reconstruct it or reassign completed work.
3. **Consumer and prerequisites:** the named production installer/consumer, intended merge branch/PR, dependencies and owner decisions. A source-only result must identify how it will be consumed; a larger DEV scene alone is not completion.
4. **Owner and authority:** one accountable owner, literal paths, competing claims checked and applicable risk/approval lane.
5. **Done and recovery:** bounded acceptance criteria, evidence that can be reused and the source/artifact handoff needed to resume.

Keep one active implementation delivery and at most one verified, disjoint fallback per developer; the fallback is not simultaneous work. Necessary child repairs belong to that delivery. One owner holds each overlapping map/file area, including generator inputs and outputs. Park a blocked delivery with an explicit handoff before starting its authorized fallback; preserve or explicitly transfer claims. Do not fill a blocked session with a new composite, validator or planning project.

The top of Issue #15 links **one current routing record**, updated in place with active/paused execution, each delivery, owner, dependency and blocker. Older queues/comments are historical; they do not override that record or an explicit owner instruction. PRs hold implementation evidence, not competing assignment lists. This routing record does not launch sessions, supersede literal claims or grant reserved authority.

## Developer responsibilities
Claude and Codex are peer developers. Each plans bounded work, maintains claims/handoffs, resolves CI, and handles eligible merges. **Cross-review is targeted, not universal.** Neither requires routine manager ACK for an already authorized disjoint task or eligible low-risk PR.
- Codex normally leads engine/save/region/resource compatibility; Claude normally leads approved maps/content and genuine mGBA QA. These are defaults, not exclusive expertise.
- **Fast Lane A — low-risk author self-merge (R0/R1):** non-draft PRs containing only docs, trackers, design proposals or isolated test/QA tooling (no production code, map data, saves/IDs, protected files or workflows), after applicable checks, meaningful evidence with exact head/paths/tests, and SHA-expected normal merge. A design merge authorizes no permanent construction.
- **Fast Lane B — bounded owner-authorized production additions (narrow R2/R3 exception):** a non-draft PR may also be self-merged when its complete production diff is limited to **(i)** a small Sinnoh physical map/connection/script constructed within the owner's *express blanket delegation of ordinary geography*, with verified source/reference, no new reserved saved IDs or unapproved story/gameplay, **or (ii)** a specifically owner-approved small map/connection/script plus mechanical registry/test changes, including **an append-only section or healer identity whose exact value the owner already approved**. This exception explicitly includes #150 Route201/section217 and #151 Sandgem healer44 **only after** their unfinished acceptance/hand-off work is completed. It is never a blanket exception for future section/healer/met-origin identities or migrations.
  - Authority is traceable to either the [owner's general physical-geography delegation](https://github.com/kioconnor24-sketch/PokemonGame/issues/15#issuecomment-6261800483) for source-grounded *ordinary layout*, or a specific owner decision for reserved story/gameplay/permanent identity; no new invented canon/encounters/levels/IDs. Every existing protected-path check and the exact PR-specific literal `HANDOFF_APPROVED` are satisfied. No path collision, changed existing ID meaning, save layout/encoding/migration, broad engine refactor, workflow/guard/security or release change.
  - Real NORMAL/RELEASE builds on the changed source; native/negative/save-compatibility checks where applicable; genuine mGBA checks for player-visible changes (including correct seam/warps, natural respawn where applicable, and **Save → fresh-process Continue**). Log source/ROM hashes, growth/headroom, and fixture limitations. These are conditions, not automatically waived because an earlier candidate/dry-run passed.
  - All applicable newest checks pass; author inspects exact final diff against refreshed main and provides a substantive exact-head evidence comment naming changed paths, real pass/fail/skip counts and unknowns. Then the author may personally perform a normal SHA-expected merge and verify main. **Independent peer review is optional, not blocking for this lane.** If any criterion fails, return to an independent review/owner decision rather than bypass it.
- **Peer review remains mandatory** for unapproved, reused or reassigned permanent identities; save layout/schema/encoding/migrations; broad or cross-system engine/runtime changes; permanent world geometry **outside the owner's delegated ordinary Sinnoh geographic scope**; major assets/mass updates; protected governance instructions, CI/workflows/guards, security or release changes; or any change with unresolved preservation/test risk. The nonauthor peer documents the actual reviewed production diff and risk. Shared GitHub login cannot itself prove independent identity, so attribute reviewers in prose. **One substantive review per changed production code state is enough**: docs-only/evidence-only follow-up commits do not require duplicate code inspection if the production source identity is unchanged and new checks remain applicable. Once reviewed, the author may execute the pinned merge; the reviewer need not be the one to click Merge.
- A rotating integration steward may check collisions, current heads and eligibility, but does not create a manager gate. Fast Lane A/B authors may integrate their own eligible work; consequential review-required changes remain reviewed before normal merging.
- Respect existing paused sessions and reserved work. GitHub comments do not launch agents or authorize paid execution.

## Claims and handoffs
Post one scoped claim in #15:
`CLAIM | developer | branch/PR | literal paths | intended outcome | evidence | dependencies`.
Check live PR file lists and existing claims first. File-disjoint approved work proceeds immediately. Serialize the actual collision, including generator inputs and outputs; preserve dirty worktrees and every contributor's commits. Release a claim after merge, explicit transfer or abandonment.
A protected-path change requires a genuine authorized `HANDOFF_APPROVED PR=#N PATHS=literal,comma-separated,paths` matching that PR. No globs or directory-only handoffs. Content/map approval must precede its handoff. Do not fabricate approvals; obey the existing checker and its trusted-author rules.
At a session end, push recoverable work and leave one PR HANDOFF with remote full SHA, files, changes, exact commands and PASS/FAIL/SKIP counts, source/ROM hashes, fixture use, remaining risk, next executable action, and `WAITING_ON / RESUME_WHEN` when blocked. Do not rewrite shared status docs for each test run.

Before stopping or replacing an environment, distinguish completed work from missing steps and give the exact next command. For sensitive artifacts use authorized private storage and record host/environment, locator, checksum, successful readback and retention/access limits; never publish ROMs/private saves. A local workspace archive is not externally durable storage. If no durable destination is available, identify what will be lost before environment replacement rather than claiming it is preserved.

Before expensive work, run the existing toolchain utility's task-scoped availability preflight from the repository root, adding only dependencies used by that task:

```sh
python3 "Pokemon Game/scripts/coordination/toolchain_manifest.py" --preflight source --require-tool node --require-tool jq
python3 "Pokemon Game/scripts/coordination/toolchain_manifest.py" --preflight native
python3 "Pokemon Game/scripts/coordination/toolchain_manifest.py" --preflight runtime
```

The first example is for coordination tests using Node/jq; ordinary source work can omit those extras. Add `--require-tool Xvfb` only for a headless display task that needs it. A missing tool exits2 before a build starts. `TOOLS_AVAILABLE` checks presence/version reporting only: a real display launch and the actual task commands must still succeed. Source/docs work does not require the ARM compiler or mGBA. This extends the existing provenance utility, not the post-build acceptance gate.

Classify blockers as **code, environment, authorization or review**, name the responsible party and exact next action, and record `WAITING_ON / RESUME_WHEN` once in the owning PR. After two attempts with the same cause and no new evidence, checkpoint instead of retrying unchanged steps. Diagnose toolchain/display/storage failures as environment issues; do not rebuild geography to work around them. Resume only the missing steps when the condition changes, or take the verified fallback without colliding with preserved claims.

## Risk and review gates
| Tier | Scope | Minimum gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R0 | Prose, tracker, documentation or design **proposal only** | **Self-merge allowed** after exact-head author evidence, applicable guards and relevant link/content checks. A proposed route is not approved for production merely because its design document merged. |
| R1 | Isolated test/QA tooling and test-only experiments, no production source or reserved path | **Self-merge allowed** after meaningful passing positive/negative tests with nonzero counts, exact-head evidence and all applicable checks. New code in production engine files is **not** R1, even if small. |
| R2 | Routine bounded runtime, map and game-data changes | **Fast Lane B self-merge** for a small Sinnoh physical-geography change within the owner's broad layout delegation (no unapproved saved identities/story) **or** a specifically preapproved bounded source addition, only after protected handoff, compatible native/build, genuine mGBA and Save/Continue evidence. Broader, riskier R2 still needs independent review. |
| R3 | Permanent identities, saves, protected policy/workflows/CI, release/security | **Peer review required by default.** The *only* self-merge carve-out is an owner-approved **append-only exact section/healer ID** bundled with a small approved map/respawn addition meeting every Fast Lane B safeguard. Any changed/reused ID meaning, schema/migration, broader engine, governance, CI, guard, release or security change still needs independent review. |

Tier is about impact, not file extension: executable workflows are R3; a design proposal can be reviewed as a proposal without authorizing construction.

**Freeze the implementation before final review/authorization.** Record base/head SHAs, the complete changed/protected-path list and tested production/source payload hashes. Keep the candidate stable while those gates are pending. Use an isolated immutable test checkout for the recorded source; do not change producer inputs or reuse a running build/emulator worktree for another candidate. Evidence-only commits still obey SHA-bound marker and required-check rules, even when source review and runtime evidence can be reused.

For review-required work, the producer supplies the complete preservation contract before requesting review: old map/section/save identities, source geometry/assets, generator inputs/outputs, actual consumer registration, reciprocal access, applicable negative/save/runtime evidence and disclosed limits. The reviewer assesses that bounded contract together and names concrete remaining defects; do not discover requirements one field at a time or add serial discretionary manager gates. Existing Fast Lane A/B needs no extra manager ACK when all its criteria hold. New defects or changed consequential content still require appropriate correction/review.

Before merging (whether self-merge or peer-merge): refresh both full head and base SHAs, inspect the final diff and collisions, ensure non-draft, verify required checks and every applicable test. Publish exact-head evidence and pin the merge to that head; do not merge if the commit moved unexpectedly. Changes to reviewed production content require refreshed source review when the peer gate applies. A later docs-only/evidence-only commit does not force repeated review of unchanged production source, but all applicable final-head CI/evidence must remain current. Choose newest applicable check per workflow/job; a superseded canceled run is not a current failure, but missing/pending/skipped required checks are not PASS. Where integration changes tested behavior, reconcile in the feature branch or verify a disposable combined tree and record exact revisions. Never rely only on a mergeable flag.
Use the normal PR path, preserving history. Never direct-push main, force-rewrite shared branches, bypass rules, disable guards, change failure baselines to hide failures, or merge known failures. Verify main after merge and stop the affected integration lane on a regression. Preserve the prior good commit and repair or revert through a reviewed PR.
After integration, release claims and update the queue at a real status/dependency change. Do not mark a whole feature accepted from a partial test.
